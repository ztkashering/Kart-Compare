Privacy & SMS policy

What Kart Compare collects when you sign up for deal alerts, and exactly how texting and email work.

What we collect

If you sign up for deal alerts, we collect whatever you enter into that form: your name (optional), email address, and phone number. That's it — we don't collect anything else about you, and this site has no accounts, passwords, or tracking of what you personally browse.

How it's used

Your email and/or phone number are used only to let you know about new grocery deals on Kart Compare. We don't sell, rent, or share your information with any third party, and we don't use it for anything beyond sending you deal updates.

Text message (SMS) terms

By checking the SMS box on the signup form, you agree to receive recurring automated marketing text messages from Kart Compare at the phone number you provide. Consent to receive texts is not a condition of any purchase or of using this site.

  • Message frequency varies depending on how often new deals are posted.
  • Message and data rates may apply, depending on your phone plan.
  • Reply STOP to any text at any time to cancel — you'll be unsubscribed immediately and won't receive another marketing text from us.
  • Reply HELP for help, or contact us directly (below).
  • Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Email terms

Checking the email box means you're opting in to occasional emails about new deals. Every email includes a way to unsubscribe, or you can just email us directly (below) and we'll remove you.

How to opt out or ask us to delete your info

Reply STOP to any text, use the unsubscribe link in any email, or email us directly and we'll take care of it right away.
